# Medical Sources Helper Bot — Ready-to-use launch copy

## Telegram launch post

🤖 Meet Medical Sources Helper Bot

A focused Telegram tool for accessing selected pediatric medication records.

Depending on the available record, you may find:
- Dosage forms
- Dosing and indications
- Pregnancy information
- Breastfeeding information

📚 The current database contains selected records based on *The Harriet Lane Handbook, 23rd Edition*. It is NOT the complete handbook and does not include every medicine.

Use it as a supplementary reference. Always verify important decisions against authoritative sources, local guidance, and clinical judgment.

➡️ Try the bot: https://t.me/MedicalSourcesHelperBot
