# Medical Sources Helper Bot — Launch Campaign Pack
**Campaign objective:** Introduce the bot to relevant healthcare professionals, drive qualified starts, and convert genuinely useful trial experiences into paid subscriptions.
**Review status:** Draft for administrator review. Do not publish automatically to public channels without human approval.

---

## 1. Core positioning

**A focused drug reference for selected pediatric medication records — with its scope made clear.**

Medical Sources Helper Bot provides quick access to selected stored drug records based on *The Harriet Lane Handbook, 23rd Edition*. Depending on the available record, information may include dosage forms, dosing and indications, and pregnancy or breastfeeding notes.

**Important scope statement:** The bot currently contains selected records only. It does not reproduce the entire handbook, does not cover every medicine, and should be used as a supplementary reference—not as a substitute for clinical judgment or checking the original source.

## 7. Community participation plan

1. Identify professional pediatric, pharmacy, and medical-education communities whose rules permit relevant tool announcements.
2. Read each community's promotion and disclosure rules before posting.
3. Contribute useful, non-promotional answers first; share the bot only when directly relevant and permitted.
4. Disclose that the bot is a project and that its database contains selected records.
5. Do not send unsolicited bulk direct messages, automate comments, create fake engagement, or repeatedly repost.
6. Track which permitted communities generate meaningful bot starts and trial engagement.

## 9. Measurement plan

Track weekly:
- Bot starts and unique users
- Free-trial activations
- Trial-to-paid conversion
- Active paid subscriptions
- Renewal and cancellation rates
- Acquisition source, where it can be measured without collecting unnecessary personal data
- User-reported missing records and usability issues

Prioritize qualified users and retention over raw impressions. Do not claim clinical outcomes or accuracy improvements without appropriate evidence.
