# Directory submission checklist

Before submitting the bot to any directory:

1. Confirm the directory accepts Telegram tools and medical reference products.
2. Read its current submission and advertising policy.
3. Use an accurate description and disclose that coverage is limited to selected stored records.
4. Do not claim the bot is an official product of Elsevier or the publisher of the handbook.
5. Do not claim clinical validation, completeness, or regulatory approval unless independently documented.
6. Submit manually and retain the directory's confirmation.
