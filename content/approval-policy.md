# Human approval policy

This project prepares drafts and research notes. It must not publish or message anyone automatically.

- No unsolicited direct messages.
- No spam, fake accounts, fake testimonials, or engagement manipulation.
- Respect each community's self-promotion and medical-content rules.
- Disclose the operator's relationship to the bot.
- Do not make unsupported claims about clinical accuracy, completeness, or outcomes.
- Never imply that the bot covers the complete Harriet Lane Handbook.
- Verify medical statements with current authoritative sources before publication.
- Do not submit patient information, private messages, credentials, or secrets to this repository.
- A human must approve every post, directory submission, and outreach message.
